Answer: "No Ma'am. Section 1.6 of the ASAC Process Manual mandates that Design Project 1 is strictly software-based. Our pipeline ingests historical BESCOM utility records, academic ERP timetable dumps, and astronomical solar irradiance curves for Bengaluru coordinates (12.845° N, 77.684° E). The API contracts are structured so physical hardware can plug in seamlessly during Phase 2."

Q: Where did you get the campus layout and measurements?
Answer: "We mapped the coordinates from open satellite imagery and official university site plans, rendering the structures as clean 2D vector layouts in SVG so the entire 60-acre campus loads in under 50 kilobytes with zero external 3D engine overhead."

Q: How does the attendance bunk calculator help campus resource management?
Answer: "It correlates student attendance density directly with classroom space utilization. By knowing attendance patterns and subject debarment risks, the campus facility manager can dynamically consolidate low-attendance elective batches into smaller seminar rooms, shutting down large 120-seat lecture halls to save substantial HVAC energy."

Q: What is left for Review 3 and final submission?
